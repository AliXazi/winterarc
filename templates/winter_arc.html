Minimal. Austere. Execution over aesthetics. Built for the Winter Arc — focus, discipline, execution.

Choose Your Arc

Standard is 90 days — the classic Winter Arc. Or set a custom countdown (7–365 days). This affects your DAY X OF Y display, streak target and arc progress bar.

Initialize Protocol — Level 1

Self-assess your baseline 1–10 across current stats. This sets your starting polygon. Be honest — you’ll level up from here.

Manage Stats

Rename, remove, or add stats. Chart, onboarding, and task stat dropdown update instantly. Deleting a stat removes it from polygon but keeps tasks (reassign). Add stat creates key from label.

Value 1–10, default 5 — you set starting polygon value.
